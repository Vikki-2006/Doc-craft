How it works

Three steps to done.

01 — Upload

Drop your files

Drag and drop PDFs or images directly into any tool workspace. No account, no setup, no waiting.

02 — Process

We handle it locally

DocCraft processes your files using PyMuPDF on your own machine. Every operation completes in seconds.

03 — Download

Your file, instantly

Download the processed file directly to your machine. No email, no cloud storage, no links that expire.

Your documents stay yours.

—

100% Local Processing

Every operation runs on your machine. Your documents never touch a server, a cloud or a third-party API. Complete privacy by architecture.

—

No Account Required

No login, no email, no tracking cookies. Open the app, use it, close it. That's the full lifecycle of your data.

—

Fully Offline Capable

Once running, DocCraft requires no internet connection. Perfect for confidential documents that cannot leave your network.
